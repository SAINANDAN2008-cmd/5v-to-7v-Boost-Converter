\# Accessibility



\## Our Commitment



We aim to make the documentation and digital resources associated with this project understandable and accessible to as many people as reasonably possible.



\## Accessibility Considerations



The project documentation aims to:



\- Use clear and simple technical explanations.

\- Organize information using headings and sections.

\- Provide meaningful descriptions of diagrams and results where practical.

\- Use readable formatting.

\- Avoid unnecessary visual complexity.



\## Known Limitations



Some simulation plots, circuit diagrams, screenshots, and technical figures may require visual interpretation and may not be fully accessible to all users.



\## Reporting Accessibility Barriers



If you encounter an accessibility barrier in the documentation or digital resources of this project, please open an issue describing:



\- The affected resource

\- The accessibility problem

\- How the problem affects usability

\- Any suggested improvement



We will consider accessibility improvements where reasonably possible.
